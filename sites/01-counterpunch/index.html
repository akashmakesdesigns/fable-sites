A counterpunch is the tool a punchcutter makes first.

          
It is struck into the end of a steel bar to form the hollow inside a letter, the counter, before any of the outside is filed away. Get the inside wrong and no amount of work on the outline will save the letter. We named the foundry after it because that is still how we draw: the white shapes first.
