One night, hour by hour

  1. 19:40

    Sunset from the terrace

    A pisco sour and the green flash, if the horizon is clean. White lights go off across the site at 20:15.

  2. 21:00

    The sky without a telescope

    Forty minutes on a reclining bench while your eyes adjust. Our guides, Inés and Tomás, point out the Southern Cross, the Coalsack and both Magellanic Clouds.

  3. 22:30

    The 60 cm telescope

    Four objects, chosen for the month. Tonight's list is on the board in the dining room by six.

  4. 01:00

    Your own photograph

    Bring a camera or borrow one of ours. We track the mount, you choose the frame, and you leave with the raw files.

  5. 05:20

    Zodiacal light, then bed

    A faint cone of dust-lit sky before dawn. Breakfast runs until noon because nobody here gets up early.

Six cabins, three kinds

Every cabin has a wood stove, blackout shutters for sleeping past sunrise, and a red reading lamp.
